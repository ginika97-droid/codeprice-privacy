CodePrice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

CodePrice is a Chrome extension that estimates a product price after an advertised coupon code. It is published by Ginika Chukwuazi, based in the United Kingdom. This notice applies to the extension wherever it is available.

What the extension accesses

When you open CodePrice or choose “Check Page,” it reads page text and product-price information from the active tab to identify a product price, currency, advertised coupon, and relevant offer terms. The scan is run on your device in your browser. CodePrice does not scan tabs in the background or access your browsing history.

How information is used and stored

Page information is used only to calculate and display the estimate you requested. The extension does not send page text, prices, coupon codes, or browsing activity to the developer, a CodePrice server, an analytics provider, an advertising service, or another third party. The scan result remains in the open popup and is discarded when the popup closes; scanned page information is not retained.

If you choose a colour theme, CodePrice saves that theme preference in Chrome's local extension storage so it can remember your choice next time. This preference stays in your browser profile and is not sent to the developer. It can be changed in the popup or removed by uninstalling the extension.

Permissions

  • activeTab: lets CodePrice read the page you chose to scan, after you open the popup.
  • scripting: runs the local price scan in that active tab.
  • storage: saves your selected colour theme locally.

Lawful basis and sharing

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies to personal data processed by CodePrice, we rely on legitimate interests in providing the user-initiated price-estimation feature. Processing is confined to the scan you request and takes place locally in your browser. CodePrice does not sell, share, or disclose scanned page information or your theme choice to the developer or other parties. The extension does not make transfers of this information to another country.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

CodePrice's use of webpage content and related information is limited to providing its disclosed price-estimation feature and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. CodePrice does not use this information for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or sale to data brokers.

Your choices and rights

You can choose another theme at any time. You can remove the locally saved theme preference by uninstalling CodePrice. If you have a privacy question or believe data-protection rights apply to information processed by the extension, contact the publisher using the email below. Depending on your location and circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, or object to processing. Because CodePrice does not receive or retain scanned page information, we may not hold a copy of that information to retrieve. UK users can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office; people in the EEA can contact their local data-protection authority.
